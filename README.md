# JP³ — Jogging in Place × at Your Own Pace × in Your Own Place

Site and content for the **JP³** protocol by Perig Gouanvic.

**Site:** https://periggouanvic.github.io/jp3/

## What is JP³?

A minimalist exercise protocol — jogging in place, at your own pace, in your own place — designed to eliminate ~80% of traditional exercise barriers, especially for vulnerable populations (chronic fatigue, depression, obesity, social anxiety, aging frailty).
